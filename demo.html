02 / THE TRADE-OFF

Four structures.
One clear payout comparison.

Flat Rate sets the baseline. Door Split scales with ticket sales. Hybrid adds a base payment to a separate share.

Best automatically chooses the higher eligible payout. Profit sliders update the terms so the owner can compare the result.

The owner sees the downside alongside the headline return.
